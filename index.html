LAMAudiobook

Python★ 1 · 72 commits

Turns an ebook into a narrated audiobook. Upload an EPUB or TXT file and a multi-agent pipeline parses it into chapters, cleans the text, detects the mood of each passage, picks a matching voice, and synthesizes speech. Output is MP3/WAV audio or an EPUB3 book with the audio built in. Voices can be cloned from a short uploaded sample.

VoiceStudy Assistant

Jupyter · Python41 commits

A Vietnamese voice assistant for schedules and notes. Every request first identifies and verifies the speaker from their voice; only then is the audio transcribed and acted on. Private notes need a second check: a spoken secret phrase that matches a salted hash stored at enrollment.

  2. Speaker ID (ECAPA-TDNN)

  5. Intent (NLU)

Vietnamese News QA with RAG

Python★ 1 · course project

A question-answering system over Vietnamese online news. Answers come with their source articles, and the system is designed to say when the corpus doesn't hold enough evidence instead of guessing. The study compares retrieval methods with Recall@k, MRR and nDCG.
