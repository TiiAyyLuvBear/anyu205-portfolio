How it works

Python★ 1 · 72 commits

Turns an ebook into a narrated audiobook. Upload an EPUB or TXT file and a multi-agent pipeline parses it into chapters, cleans the text, detects the mood of each passage, picks a matching voice, and synthesizes speech. Output is MP3/WAV audio or an EPUB3 book with the audio built in. Voices can be cloned from a short uploaded sample.

  1. Upload book
  2. Parser
  3. Text cleaner
  4. Mood detection
  5. Voice selection
  6. TTS
  7. Quality check
  8. MP3 / EPUB3
  • FastAPI
  • Streamlit
  • SQLite job queue
  • VieNeu-TTS 0.3B
  • XTTSv2
